## Beam Remote 1.9.0

**Screenshots and clips.** A new camera button on the stream page's toolbar saves a screenshot of the PC's screen (Ctrl + Alt + Shift + P) or records a clip with sound (Ctrl + Alt + Shift + R), up to 10 minutes, as an MP4 that plays almost anywhere. A small "REC" label shows while recording; click it to stop. Files go to the viewing device's Downloads.

**Picture-in-picture.** Keep the stream playing in a small floating window while you do something else.

**Volume.** The sound button now has a volume slider, remembered per device. The PC's owner can set the starting volume in Browser controls.

**Pauses when you're not watching.** When the stream's tab is hidden, or nobody has touched the mouse, keyboard, screen or a controller for a while (15 minutes; change it or turn it off in Settings), the PC stops sending the picture. Sound keeps playing, and the picture is back the moment you return. Saves data on school and mobile networks, battery, and the PC's work.

**Connection test.** Quality → Test my connection measures how fast your connection to the PC is and suggests the best quality preset for it, with one click to use it.

**Data used.** The detailed stats show how much data this visit has used, handy on school or mobile networks.

Install: download `Beam-Remote-Setup-1.9.0.exe` below, or let the app update itself.
