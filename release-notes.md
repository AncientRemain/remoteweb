## Beam Remote 1.6.0

**Game controllers.** Plug a controller into the device you're watching on (USB or Bluetooth: school Chromebooks, phones, tablets, Macs, other PCs) and press a button. Games on your PC see a real Xbox controller, with vibration sent back to the controller in your hand. Up to 4 at once. Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and most others work.
- One-time setup on the PC: App & network → Controllers → Install controller driver (ViGEmBus, the signed driver Sunshine and DS4Windows use). The viewing devices need nothing installed.
- **On-screen controller** for touch screens: two sticks, A/B/X/Y, D-pad, bumpers, triggers, View and Menu.
- The new controller button in the toolbar shows each controller's player number and turns vibration on or off.

**Picture adjustments.** Brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, warmth and sharpness, with presets (Vivid, Cinema, Night, High contrast, Crisp text, Grayscale), in Settings → Picture. Done by the viewing browser, so they cost the PC and the stream nothing; each device keeps its own.

Install: download `Beam-Remote-Setup-1.6.0.exe` below, or let the app update itself.
