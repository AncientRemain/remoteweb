## Beam Remote 1.4.0

**Privacy screen.** Choose what this PC's own monitor shows while someone is streaming: normal, black, dimmed, a message, a clock, a picture or a floating logo (Security → Privacy screen). The stream always shows the real screen, because the cover is left out of screen capture. The streaming device can change it from the toolbar. Someone at the PC can press Ctrl + Alt + Shift + H to see the screen again.

Install: download `Beam-Remote-Setup-1.4.0.exe` below, or let the app update itself. Windows may warn that the app isn't signed: More info → Run anyway.
